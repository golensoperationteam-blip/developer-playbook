# Developer Playbook

## Developer Identity

This is the working knowledge base for the developer and e-commerce technology professional behind the Golens operations environment. The profile combines practical commerce operations, product-content workflows, AI-assisted work, automation, GitHub knowledge management, visual-content workflows, and growing software/architecture capability.

### Philosophy

- Practical first: technology should solve a measurable business or workflow problem.
- Structure matters: repeatable work should become documented processes.
- Automate repetition: use tools and AI to reduce manual, error-prone work.
- Evidence over assumption: undocumented personal facts must not be invented.
- Reusable knowledge: capture decisions so people and AI can apply them later.
- Continuous improvement: treat the playbook as a living system.

> Years of experience, certifications, and quantified impact are recorded only when explicitly verified.

## Core Expertise

- E-commerce operations and product-listing workflows
- Shopify, Amazon, and IndiaMART operations
- Product data, specifications, catalog quality, and marketplace content
- Digital marketing and commerce-oriented content workflows
- AI-assisted productivity, prompting, and automation
- GitHub and Markdown-based knowledge management
- Canva and visual-content workflows
- Google Sheets and structured-data operations
- AI/agent concepts and orchestration
- Process design, documentation, and operational standards
